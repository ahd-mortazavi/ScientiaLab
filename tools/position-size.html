How it works

cash at risk = account × risk % units = ⌊cash at risk ÷ |entry − stop|⌋ reward : risk = |target − entry| ÷ |entry − stop|

Direction comes from where your stop sits: below entry means long, above means short. A target on the stop's side of entry is flagged rather than scored.
